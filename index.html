John Locke ✏️

1632 – 1704

English Philosopher
Tap to Study ⤵
A+

📌 Key Ideas

  • All people are born with natural rights (life, liberty, property).
  • Proposed the social contract (government needs consent of the governed).
  • Citizens can overthrow a government that fails to protect rights.

🇺🇸 Influence on U.S.

Inspired Thomas Jefferson’s Declaration of Independence ("life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness"). Established that American government exists to serve citizens.

📖 Facts

  • Wrote Two Treatises of Government (1689).
  • Was originally a trained physician!

Montesquieu 🏫

1689 – 1755

French Judge
Tap to Study ⤵
100

📌 Key Ideas

  • Created the concept of separation of powers.
  • Introduced checks and balances between branches.
  • Believed laws must adapt to a country's specific needs.

🇺🇸 Influence on U.S.

Directly shaped the U.S. Constitution. The Framers divided the government into three branches (legislative, executive, judicial) so no single group could become tyrannical.

📖 Facts

  • Published The Spirit of the Laws (1748).
  • Inherited his title and a seat in the regional parliament of Bordeaux.

Voltaire 📓

1694 – 1778

French Writer
Tap to Study ⤵
A+

📌 Key Ideas

  • Fierce advocate for freedom of speech and religion.
  • Supported the separation of church and state.
  • Used satire and reason to criticize corruption and intolerance.

🇺🇸 Influence on U.S.

Heavily influenced James Madison and the Bill of Rights. His beliefs are the foundation of the First Amendment (freedom of speech, religion, press, and assembly).

📖 Facts

  • Wrote Treatise on Tolerance and the novel Candide.
  • Was sent to the Bastille prison twice for his outspoken writing!
